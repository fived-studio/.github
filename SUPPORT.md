# Getting Support

Hi 👋 — thanks for using something we built. Here's the fastest way to get help, depending on what you need.

## 🐛 You found a bug

Open a **bug report** on the affected repository. Include:

- What you did
- What you expected
- What actually happened (with logs, screenshots, or a minimal repro)
- Version, OS, and runtime details

A 60‑second well‑written repro is worth more than a 30‑message back‑and‑forth.

## 💡 You have a feature idea

Open a **feature request** on the relevant repo. Lead with the **problem** you're trying to solve, not the solution — we often find a better path together once we understand the user need.

## ❓ You have a question

- Check the repo's `README.md` and `docs/` first
- Search existing issues and discussions — odds are someone asked it already
- If still stuck, open a **Discussion** on the repo (preferred over issues for open‑ended questions)

## 🔒 You found a security issue

**Please don't open a public issue.** See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for private disclosure.

## 🤝 You want to partner / hire / work together

Reach out to any maintainer listed on our [org profile](https://github.com/fived-studio), or email `hello@fived.studio`.

## ⏱ Response times

We do our best to triage within a few business days. We are a small team building this alongside our day jobs — please be patient, and feel free to nudge politely if a thread goes quiet for more than a week.
